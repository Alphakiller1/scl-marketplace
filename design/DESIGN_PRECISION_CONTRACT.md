# SCL design precision contract

This contract closes the system-level visual gaps that remain after the evidence-surface rebuilds. `SCL-DESIGN-SPEC.md` v2.0 remains authoritative.

## Type hierarchy

- The rolling hero title is exactly 38/40 on mobile and 56/56 from the desktop breakpoint.
- Public page titles use one scale: 36px mobile and 42px desktop, with a tight display line height.
- Product and authentication headings are sentence case. Uppercase remains limited to eyebrows, stamps, short board labels, and sanctioned section IDs.
- Numeric values remain Inter tabular; this pass does not change data typography.

## Interaction geometry

- Primary navigation and inspection controls are at least 44px in both dimensions where geometry permits.
- Hero previous/next and slide selectors use 44px hit areas; the dot or rail inside remains visually small.
- Ranking filters, trust-lens tabs, chart-window controls, proof-history reveal controls, and their reset/load actions are at least 44px high.
- Raising a hit area must not add a new card, shadow, or decorative fill.

## Composition guardrails

- Keep the current profile Evidence | Latest Proof desk, Picks ledger, Packages register, Discover lane index, and Rank board structures unchanged.
- Keep the owner-approved three-slide hero copy and the approved crown artwork unchanged.
- Honest empty and provisional states remain visible and direct.
- Pink remains conviction; blue remains navigation.

## Acceptance

- QA at 375, 768, 1024, 1280, and 1440px in dark and light.
- No horizontal overflow, normal-size text below 4.5:1, broken images, or mobile input zoom traps.
- Reduced motion renders a stable final state before automated contrast checks.
